# Community code of conduct

We aim for a welcoming, professional community regardless of experience, background,
identity or accessibility needs. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Discuss
technical tradeoffs on their merits; acknowledge uncertainty and mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct and disclosure of
private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or unredacted user
projects. These standards apply to issues, reviews, discussions and project events.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using GitHub's private reporting
or contact mechanisms. Maintainers will review concerns discreetly, protect reporter
privacy where possible, and may warn, remove content, limit participation or ban
contributors depending on severity. Maintainers are held to the same standards.
Do not retaliate against people who report concerns in good faith.
